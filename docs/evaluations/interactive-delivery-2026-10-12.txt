Interactive delivery follow-up — 12 October 2026

Decision

Keep memory phase one and the commitment-delivery work. Merge a narrow fix for
current-turn ownership, first-pass output room and ordinary interactive reasoning.
Memory phase two and the factual-shadow experiment remain inactive.

Changes

- The sole recipient of an admitted human DM owns a response to the current turn
  even when auxiliary intent classification fails or labels uptake optional.
  The original classification metadata is not rewritten or promoted to trusted.
- Generation, review and bounded recovery resolve the newest turn: a greeting,
  status, plan or necessary clarification is a complete response at that level.
  An unrelated remembered promise creates no new artifact obligation.
- A current text response owner can use up to 2,000 characters and 120 prose
  words (or the persona's existing larger ceiling) on its first attempt. This
  widens a maximum, not a minimum/target. Detailed/considered budgets are retained.
  Nonowner and voice limits stay bounded; mixed scenes enforce caps per actor.
- Ordinary public/DM generation explicitly requests no hidden reasoning, matching
  the existing compact conversation path. Detailed/considered requests keep their
  prior policy. Compatibility fallbacks, mandatory review and retry limits remain.

No language/subject-specific dispatch, text-matching regex, memory-storage change,
relationship-value change or permissive factual-publication fallback was added.

Mechanism check: all three candidate riddle generations returned visible output
with reasoning_effort=none and zero reasoning tokens. Two baseline first attempts
used 1,497/1,500 completion tokens on reasoning and ended at length; one ultimately
hit the evaluation deadline. This supports a tail-latency improvement in this
bounded comparison, not a claim of universal acceleration or instant conversation.

Blind qualitative grading: candidate preferred in 6/24 pairs, baseline in 2/24,
16 ties. Baseline: 16 satisfactory / 4 partial / 4 failed. Candidate:
20 satisfactory / 4 partial / 0 failed. The graders saw complete published
exchanges before version mappings, not draft/review text or timing.

Important limitations and remaining issues

- Three candidate creative multipart responses remained vague. One candidate
  riddle muddled the wording of its clue. Publication is not proof of completeness.
- A minimal array-copy example was graded more strictly by one reviewer than by
  another. The archive preserves each rationale; counts are qualitative judgments,
  not a statistically established quality score or demand for more code lines.
- Greeting, deferred-status and missing-input controls stayed brief/on scope.
  Human-owned-photo recall improved from three silent completions to three replies.
- Correct-code and correct-riddle fixed drafts passed 3/3 in both snapshots;
  renewed-promise drafts were rejected 3/3 in both. The known incorrect sequential
  array comment still passed 3/3 in BOTH. No factual-review improvement is claimed.
- Six current-only public/ambient smoke cells all returned reviewed replies,
  without errors or retries (12 calls, concurrency two, median 5.1 seconds).
  Programming replies were correct. Film recommendations stayed vague and the
  autonomous replies were shallow. This was not an A/B or long-run scheduler test.
- Frozen memory, disabled research/extraction and zero DM debounce isolate delivery;
  tests do not establish webfetch, voice, long-run relationships or multi-user load.
