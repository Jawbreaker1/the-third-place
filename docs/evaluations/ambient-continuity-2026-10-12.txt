AMBIENT CONVERSATION CONTINUITY — LIVE GEMMA EVALUATION
2026-10-12

Recommendation
--------------
Proceed with a cautious, observable trial of final candidate V3, not a claim
that autonomous conversation quality is solved. V3 demonstrates better uptake
of new conversational material while preserving short, playful replies and a
human-directed answer. Important weaknesses remain: repetitive agreement,
academic language in technical discussion, and conversations drifting back
to broad aesthetic or philosophical theses.

No review criteria were relaxed to obtain these results. No test account,
session, message or memory was written to production rooms. All evaluation
model loops were stopped after the planned controls completed.

Method and limits
-----------------
The longitudinal harness uses four successive scheduling episodes per room,
with material A, B, C, then A again. Only production-reviewed committed turns
enter the following episode's rolling history and resident style memory.
History is not reset between episodes, and rejected drafts never become facts
in subsequent conversation. Matching transport seeds are selected by scenario,
episode, candidate and model-call position; changed prompts can still change
outputs. A new seed is an opportunity, not a mandatory topic switch.

Each room uses four fixed residents with deterministic rotated selection.
This supports reproducibility but is not the production director's broader,
stochastic roster. Nox and Bosse therefore recur particularly often in these
fixtures. Synthetic time advances between episodes; the test does not measure
actual hourly activity, full persistent relationships, live scheduling,
research retrieval, voice behavior or the complete human participant pool.

The model was google/gemma-4-26b-a4b. The real server continued using it.
Initially two evaluation lanes could overlap with up to three production
background requests. After provider context errors, evaluation was reduced
to one lane; all final short controls were sequential. This is not an unloaded
latency benchmark, and the cause of the provider errors is not proven.
Read-only model metadata reported context 65536, parallelism 4, batch 512,
physical batch 512 and Flash Attention enabled. No model settings were changed.
Successful reviews reached 16870 prompt tokens, so simply dividing 65536 by
four is not an established explanation of the failures.

Quality was assessed by reading conversations, including independent review,
not by keyword/regex grading or a quota of facts, topic changes or disagreement.
Longer replies and more publications are not automatically better. In
particular, baseline programming develops a useful cleanup/diagnostics chain;
ignoring a new seed is not itself a failure while that chain remains productive.

Longitudinal observations
-------------------------
Baseline:
- Football publishes 15 turns and discards 11 drafts. The discussion remains
  in a risk/control/lack-of-verticality frame across the new opportunities.
- 3D publishes 22 and discards 5, largely retaining shader/material/imperfection
  discussion rather than meaningfully taking up animation or pipeline ideas.
- Programming publishes 32 without a discarded draft. It develops async
  cleanup, connection pools, OS diagnostics, telemetry and logging overhead.
  This is sustained method development, although some technical claims would
  require separate verification; it is not a fact-checked programming tutorial.
- Pub publishes 19 and discards 6. The film/CGI/polygon/nostalgia exchange
  persists across new music and food opportunities, but contains real teasing.

V1 and V2 are not hidden or presented as successes:
- V1 football: 16 published, 3 discarded. There is more argument development
  but no demonstrated escape from the original frame. Several longer replies
  become academic rather than conversational.
- V1 3D: 13 published, 6 discarded. All published messages are 226–514
  characters (median 411), demonstrating a real uniform-length/style risk.
- V2 football: 13 published, 6 discarded. One opener takes up an inswinging
  corner, then returns to the same risk thesis; Muller material is not developed.
- V2 pub, first two episodes: 13 published. A real shift from film to music
  occurs, but it soon becomes simulator/system/life metaphors rather than a
  sustained concrete music exchange. The extra third-episode row is excluded.

Final V3:
- Football, three complete episodes: 18 published; another 5 published turns
  in interrupted episode four are preserved separately (23 total, 8 discarded
  drafts total). The corner exchange discusses tracking the runner versus the
  ball and active zonal defense. The next episode takes up Muller, off-ball
  movement and what scouting metrics miss. This is more than cosmetic seed
  wording, although much of the exchange is still elaborate agreement and
  broad claims about structure, data and intent. The incomplete final episode
  returns to risk/control. Median message length is 345 characters (25–602).
- Pub, four complete episodes: 20 published, 9 discarded. Film moves to music,
  then cold pizza, then older low-budget sci-fi. Replies remain colloquial and
  reasonably short: median 146 characters (55–264). The last eight published
  replies nevertheless converge again on essentially one nostalgia thesis:
  imperfect practical effects feel more authentic than polished CGI. This is
  a notable remaining loop, not eight independent developments.

No completed longitudinal variant produced an explicit model-selected empty
response. Many episodes ended after discarded drafts. Such endings must not be
described as demonstrated natural abstention or intentional social closure.

Separate failure categories
---------------------------
- Semantic repetition/peer-echo rejections remain common. The reports retain
  the rejected draft and review explanation so these can be inspected.
- V1 3D has two mechanical humanizer repairs truncated at 960 completion
  tokens, 957 of them reasoning tokens, with no usable structured answer.
  They took about 67.6 and 62.5 seconds. These are not semantic-review verdicts.
- V2 football has one unavailable review due to its 20-second deadline.
- V3 football and pub each have one unavailable review with LM Studio's
  'Context size has been exceeded' provider error. Generation had succeeded;
  no line was published after the failed review. These are not voluntary
  silence or evidence of stricter semantic standards.
- V2 pub includes a genuinely unfinished draft ('exakt, det är den där '),
  correctly not published. It should not be blamed on a conversational veto.

Short final controls
--------------------
Identical synthetic fixtures were run on baseline and V3. Unlike the long
manifest, these pre-existing short controls do not fix model RNG seeds.
They are qualitative smoke checks, not statistical non-regression proof.

Human riddle answer:
  Baseline 3/3 publications; V3 3/3. Mira explicitly recognizes the correct
  answer in both. V3: 'Rätt! Det gick ju för snabbt, jag hade hoppats på något
  svårare.' Followers respond to that exchange rather than ignoring the person.

Spanish plushie preference:
  Baseline 3 publications; V3 1 publication plus 2 explicit model-selected
  empty replies. Juno's V3 reply is playful and expresses a collection
  preference, without invented manufacturer or market facts. This is a real
  example of model abstention, unlike the long-run reviewer-induced silence.
  One brief run does not establish that the V3 discussion will be equally long.

Swedish warmth / silly low-stakes chat:
  Baseline 3 publications and 3 echo rejections; V3 6 publications, no rejection.
  V3 includes 'Orkar inte. 😂' and short teasing. Lengths are 14–105 characters,
  median 29. There is no forced lecture, factual example or declared topic
  transition. Some replies are thin agreement; that is acceptable here.

Short V3-only 3D opening:
  8/8 publications, concrete suggestions about light shape, reflections and
  sampling. But every reply is 337–491 characters, median 406. This confirms
  that technical rooms still tend toward uniformly long answers. The practical
  content does not erase that style concern, and these technical suggestions
  were not independently renderer-specific fact checked. This was one short
  episode, not a new matched long V3 3D or programming test.

No final short control had a provider error or truncated repair. Their elapsed
times should not be compared directly with the earlier concurrent long runs.

The findings support a bounded trial of improved episode continuity and topic
uptake, with observation of actual rooms and provider health. They do not
support claiming that repetitive agreement, academic style, factual accuracy
or all-room long-term naturalness are solved. No further prompt iterations or
model tests were performed after the planned final controls. Harness structural
tests passed 7/7. The main agent separately reports 2508/2508 application tests,
successful type checking and a successful build.

After all evaluation loops stopped, the main agent restarted the runtime for
manual trial. Health and model connectivity were confirmed, and the existing
ngrok tunnel was verified. No extra test user or message was added to the
production environment as part of that rollout.
