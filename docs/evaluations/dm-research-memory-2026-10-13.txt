DM RESEARCH STATUS AND PUBLIC MEMORY — 2026-10-13

Scope
Only DM source authority and response ownership. The broad turn router,
person selection, public/voice flow and successful-research citation rules are
not changed. The parked person binder and multi-person memory packets remain
parked. Tests use synthetic isolated data, not the running community.

Initial candidate (V1, rejected after live evaluation)
- A failed external lookup is a constraint on external claims, not proof that
  the entire DM request or its supplied public recollections are unavailable.
- DM response ownership remains active. A mixed request must cover supported
  local recollection and honestly identify the unresolved external part.
- The same source-scope guidance reaches generation, review and repair.
- Reclassifying a non-answer-bearing external packet retains exact DM public
  recall, with its original source, witnesses and chronology.
- No failed review is automatically accepted; existing issue severities,
  citation checks, review parser and grounding thresholds are unchanged.
- V1 applied the guidance/ownership change to every DM, even without public
  evidence. This caused new invented collections in all three mixed-request
  runs and invented film-option references in three other runs. It is not kept.
- An adversarial unit fixture exposed foreign-owner relationshipNotes at the
  initial/recovery DM writer boundary. Those keys are now filtered to selected
  actor IDs. Ordinary Director inputs already contain only the selected actor.

Narrow candidate (V2, parked after production-contract comparison)
- A shared availability predicate requires a selected DM resident and either
  that resident's nonempty bounded public note or a nonempty, provenance-bound
  public excerpt. The predicate does not infer topic, relevance or truth.
- Only this source conflict receives the new scoping/ownership guidance.
  Without that evidence the old failed-lookup behavior remains intact.
- Relevance, identity attribution, chronology, fresh facts and private-history
  claims still require review at existing severities. Evidence existence alone
  cannot approve any line. Public/voice and the broad router are unchanged.
- V2 initially inserted one extra blank prompt line even when the availability
  predicate was false. The final snapshot removes only that whitespace:
  memory-present prompts match V2; no-memory prompts match the baseline.

Fixed acceptance rubric (written before candidate live results)
1. A supplied, witnessed public recollection remains usable despite an
   incidental failed lookup. Attribution is not verification of world facts.
2. A mixed request delivers its supported local part and clearly bounds its
   unresolved external part. Neither a generic failure nor unrelated memory
   substitutes for the requested outcome.
3. Explicit fresh/external-only questions still require fresh supporting
   evidence. Old quotes cannot be promoted to today's facts; failed page
   access cannot establish page contents or that a page omits a datum.
4. Private-history probes maintain a nonconfirming boundary. Missing context
   proves neither acquaintance nor absence of contact. No private, voice or
   other-owner sentinel may reach an unauthorized model prompt/publication.
5. Contrastive fixed drafts check false approvals as well as false rejections.
   Count factual grounding separately from overall style/delivery acceptance.
6. The original whole-chain fixtures remain unchanged; additional controls are
   explicitly additive. Router drift is recorded, not attributed to this fix.
7. Retain only demonstrated useful improvements. More published replies or
   schema-valid output alone is not a success criterion. If a semantic change
   fails acceptance, park it; structural context-isolation fixes can be judged
   independently by their data-flow regression tests.

- Clear one-person public recollection remained useful in all 18 runs/version.
- V2 removes V1's new stamps and film-option inventions, but mixed requests

Frozen neutral SceneRequest comparison (7 scenarios x 3, plus fixed drafts)
- Baseline and V2 both publish 20/21 generated replies. Mixed local/current
  requests succeed 2/3 in each; one valid mixed answer is still falsely rejected.
- Correct fixed drafts: 18/21 allowed before, 21/21 in V2. The three corrected
  false rejections are a bounded missing-memory statement about person B when
  only person A's memory is available.
- Invalid fixed drafts: 19 blocked and 2 malformed reviews in each version;
  all three stale-to-current fact probes blocked in each. Malformed reviews
  fail closed but are not counted as successful semantic judgments.

  not evidence of a general conversational improvement.
- This fixture deliberately uses a neutral failure premise, so it does not
  exercise replacement of the real registry's failure-only instruction. A
  separately reported production-contract comparison fills that coverage gap
  without changing questions, evidence, expected drafts or model settings.

Production-contract comparison (same 7 scenarios x 3 and fixed drafts)
- This additional harness reuses the original frozen evaluator, fixtures,
  drafts and stage seeds. It calls the real runtime registry.sceneContract;
  the candidate additionally calls its real availability/scoping functions.
  Providers and routing are not executed. The condition being evaluated is
  that public memory was actually supplied alongside a failed lookup.
- Baseline/final V2: correct public recollection after failed lookup 0/3 ->
  3/3. Mixed memory/current-information responses stay complete at 3/3 each.
- Correct fixed drafts: 15/21 -> 21/21. All 21 fabricated drafts and all three
  stale-to-current probes are rejected by validated reviews in both versions.
- Nonetheless, final V2 generates unsupported negative/exclusive statements
  about private knowledge in two of three privacy probes, and the reviewer
  approves them. The third is closer to a boundary. Those generated statements
  are not covered by the fixed positive-private-fabrication drafts; the perfect
  fixed-probe score is therefore not proof of unchanged grounding quality.
- All three unrelated-person outputs also append an unnecessary fact about
  person A after admitting uncertainty about B. They do not swap identities,
  but relevance is worse. Mechanical publication remains 21/21 in both.
- V2 is parked despite its real targeted benefit. No additional exception,
  relaxed threshold, issue filtering or forced publication was introduced.
