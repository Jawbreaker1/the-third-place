METHOD
Frozen source copies; real google/gemma-4-26b-a4b via LmStudioBackend; production
stopped; maximum 2 concurrent model calls. All fixtures/data are synthetic.
V1: 72 cells, 116 calls. V2: 94 cells, 158 calls. Total: 166 cells, 274 calls.

Three repeats per routing/fixed-review probe, two per public conversational
scenario. Real Director -> generation -> review -> durable-publication path.
No production logins, room/message writes or memory changes. Public fixtures
disable background extraction, autonomous continuation and delayed recovery;
research execution deliberately fails rather than accessing the internet.
Positive current-price/URL controls test routing, not external retrieval.

One candidate bird-correction turn still chose unnecessary research and only

CONCLUSION
Supported incremental improvement in the targeted failure boundaries, with
factual/privacy/duplicate controls intact. Not proof that all silence or
unnecessary research is solved. No conclusions about voice, long-run autonomy,
real research providers, multi-human load or universal reviewer accuracy.
